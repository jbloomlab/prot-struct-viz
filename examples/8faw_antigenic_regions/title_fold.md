## Influenza H3 hemagglutinin: the fold beneath the antigenic regions

The same coloring as the surface view, drawn as a cartoon. The five classical
antigenic regions of HA1 are patches on the surface; here you can see where they
sit on the fold, and how the LSTc receptor analogue (black) reaches into the
binding site at the membrane-distal tip of each protomer.

Switching views does not move the camera, so a site framed in one view stays
framed in the other.
