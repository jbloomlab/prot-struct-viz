# Sixteen years of antigenic drift in influenza H3 hemagglutinin

The same trimer, now with the antigenic-region coloring dropped. **Red** marks every site
that differs between the HA of A/Perth/16/2009 — the H3N2 component of the 2010-2011
northern-hemisphere vaccine — and the HA of **subclade K**, represented by
A/Darwin/1415/2025. Everything else is uniform gray; the black receptor analogue is kept as
a landmark.

The two HAs differ at 43 sites across the ectodomain, and **40 of those are modeled here**
(`K2N` and `L3I` fall before the first modeled residue, `T328A` after the last). Hover any
red patch for the substitution — `N145S_HA1`, `I77V_HA2`.

**27 of the 40 fall inside the five antigenic regions** the first two views colored: eight
in site A, nine in site B, four in site C, two in site D, four in site E. That is the point
of putting these views in this order. Sixteen years of drift is not spread over the
molecule; it lands on the surface an antibody has to bind, and it lands there far out of
proportion to the fraction of the protein those regions make up.
