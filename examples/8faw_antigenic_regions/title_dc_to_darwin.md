# One vaccine update in influenza H3 hemagglutinin

The same comparison as the previous view against the same target, but over one season
instead of sixteen years. **Red** marks every site that differs between the two most recent
H3N2 vaccine strains:

- A/District_Of_Columbia/27/2023, subclade J.2 — the 2025-2026 vaccine;
- A/Darwin/1415/2025, subclade K — the 2026-2027 vaccine.

They differ at 10 sites, **8 of them modeled here** (`K2N` and `T328A` are outside what
8FAW models). Six of the eight — 135, 144 and 145 in site A, and 158, 160 and 189 in site
B — are in an antigenic region, and all six sit on the membrane-distal tip beside the
receptor-binding site, which is why a handful of substitutions is enough to warrant
changing the vaccine.

Compare the red here with the red in the previous view: this is what one antigenic
step looks like beside sixteen years of them.

Sequences as deposited: [PV280355](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nuccore/PV280355) and
[PX422923](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nuccore/PX422923).
