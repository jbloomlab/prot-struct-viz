## Antigenic regions of influenza H1 HA

Uncleaved HA from influenza A/Victoria/2570/2019, the egg-derived A(H1N1)pdm09 component of the
2021 vaccine ([PDB 9GSP](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/9GSP)). HA1 is
colored by the classical antigenic region each residue belongs to, taken from Table 2 of
[Wilson et al. (2015), *Virology* 485:252-62](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5737639/),
which carries the regions Caton and colleagues mapped on A/PR/8/34 into the numbering of the
2009 pandemic lineage:

- **indigo** — site Sa
- **cyan** — site Sb
- **green** — site Ca1
- **purple** — site Ca2
- **teal** — site Cb
- **light gray** — HA1, not in a defined antigenic region
- **mid gray** — HA2

Glycans are hidden.

Every residue carries its site in HA1 or HA2 numbering — `192_HA1`, `14_HA2` — shown on
mouseover.

This is an **HA0**: the protease site that would cut HA1 from HA2 is uncut, and the loop
carrying it — HA1 324-327 and HA2 1-6 — is disordered, so it is missing from every view here
rather than hidden by it.
