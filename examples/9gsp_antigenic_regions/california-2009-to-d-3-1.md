## HA mutations between a 2009 and 2025 H1N1 strain

Structure of H1N1 HA ([PDB 9GSP](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/9GSP)) with red indicating every site that differs between
A/California/07/2009 (the 2009 pandemic vaccine strain)
and the subclade D.3.1 strain
A/Missouri/11/2025 (the H1N1pdm09 component of the 2026-2027 vaccine).
Sites that did not change shown in gray.

12 of the 38 ectodomain mutations are at sites in the classically defined antigenic regions: five
in site Sa, three in site Sb, one in site Ca1, two in site Ca2, one in site Cb. All 12 are among
the 37 at sites in the structure shown here.

The HA sequences for the two strains are [FJ966974](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nuccore/FJ966974) and
[PV886191](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nuccore/PV886191).
