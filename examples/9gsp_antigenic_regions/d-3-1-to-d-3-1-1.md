## HA mutations between H1N1 subclades D.3.1 and D.3.1.1

Structure of H1N1 HA ([PDB 9GSP](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/9GSP)) with red indicating every site that differs between
subclade D.3.1 (A/Missouri/11/2025, the H1N1pdm09 component of the 2026-2027 vaccine)
and subclade D.3.1.1 (A/Andalucia/PMC-00977/2025, collected in 2025).
Sites that did not change shown in gray.

They differ at 4 sites:
  - One mutation in antigenic region Ca2: A139D
  - Three additional HA1 mutations outside the defined antigenic regions: R113K, E283K, K302E

The HA sequences for the two strains are [PV886191](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nuccore/PV886191) and
[PX399795](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nuccore/PX399795).
