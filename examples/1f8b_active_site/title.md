## Influenza B neuraminidase active site

The sialic-acid analogue **DANA** bound in the active site of influenza B/Beijing/1/87
neuraminidase ([PDB 1F8B](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1F8B)), with the residues
that contact it shaded by distance:

- **dark red** — direct contact (< 3 A)
- **red** — within 3.5 A
- **light red** — within 4 A

Blue sites carry insertion codes (169A, 412A, 412B), showing that author numbering
with insertion codes is addressed correctly. The green sugar is the first residue of
an N-glycan; naming it in the CSV replaces its 3D-SNFG symbol with a plain
ball-and-stick in the CSV color, while the other sugars keep their SNFG symbols.

The view shows **assembly 1**, the biological tetramer, which Mol\* generates in the
browser from four crystal symmetry operations — the file embeds only the deposited
asymmetric unit.
